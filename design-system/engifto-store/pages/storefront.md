# Engifto storefront override

Applies to home, shop, products, bag, checkout, confirmation, story, help, policies and photo credits.

The user explicitly requested preserving the existing Claude-inspired visual identity. This overrides the automated master generator's IoT/dashboard, dark-mode, single-column and alternative-font recommendations.

- Background: ivory #faf9f5; secondary surface #f5f3ed; borders #deddd4.
- Text: charcoal #24241f; secondary #64645b; accent terracotta #b45132.
- Display typography: self-hosted Newsreader, regular and italic. Body: self-hosted DM Sans.
- Editorial split hero, real workspace photography, three collections, open product grids.
- Cart and checkout: two columns on desktop, one on mobile; labeled fields and visible demo totals.
- Primary CTA: charcoal with ivory text. Subtle corners (4–6px), no heavy card shadows.
- 1200px content width; 20px mobile side margins; 55–80px section spacing.
- Product photos crop consistently; visible sample-catalog disclosure and source/license credits.
- Keyboard focus visible, SVG icons, reduced-motion support, no layout-shifting hover effects.
- Verify 375, 768, 1024 and 1440px; local images; no horizontal overflow.

User readability update: small interface text is 13–16px; product-card price 22px; detail price 36px. Card Add to cart uses a pale warm surface with a terracotta border, 44px minimum height, and a plus icon. The header counter overlaps the top-right of the bag silhouette and hides at zero. All visible cart copy uses “cart”; no prototype labels on the storefront.

Final ATC override: replace the pale rectangular button with a charcoal capsule (999px radius), ivory label and 17px bag SVG before the text; terracotta hover, 44px desktop and 46px mobile minimum height. Mobile CTA is full width beneath the price.
